Anchor bolt and foundation interview questions for site and QA/QC engineers (with answers)

By Hicham Zein Eddine · From the author's live workshop

Short answer: Interviews for site and QA/QC roles on steel-structure projects test the template method, ASTM F1554 grades, tolerances and corrections for misaligned bolts, the pre-pour final gate, cube testing and who approves field fixes. The short answers below come from the field guide.

Use these to prepare, then explain each answer with an example from your own site. Interviewers listen for the sequence and for who has authority to approve.

Questions and short answers

  1. What is an anchor bolt template for? It holds the bolt group in the base plate's exact pattern during the pour, and is kept to drill the base plates.
  2. What are the ASTM F1554 grades? Grade 36 (250 MPa yield), 55 (380 MPa), 105 (720 MPa).
  3. Can a bolt be specified as both F1554 and ISO 8.8? No. They are separate systems; follow the drawings.
  4. How do you verify bolt diameter? With a caliper, on every bolt, yourself. 22 mm and 24 mm look identical by eye.
  5. Where do you set out anchor bolts from? Survey control and offset pegs, never from the formwork.
  6. Bolts are 4 mm out after casting. What do you do? Check the base plate first: holes are normally oversized (bolt diameter + 3-6 mm).
  7. And 10 mm out? Consult the structural engineer; slotted holes with thick hardened plate washers may be permitted.
  8. And 20 mm out? Do not bend the bolts. With the structural engineer's approval, cut them and install post-installed anchors next to them.
  9. Can you heat a bolt to bend it into place? Not without the structural engineer's approval; bending and heating reduce capacity.
  10. What is the final gate? The pre-concrete inspection of bolts, rebar, formwork, levels and cleanliness, signed by the consultant or client. No signature, no pour.
  11. Blow or wash first? Blow out debris with compressed air, then wash. Washing first makes mud.
  12. How do you protect threads during the pour? Masking tape or caps, then caps and marking until erection.
  13. How many cubes per sample, and when are they broken? Typically 3, at 7, 14 and 28 days (BS EN 12390), per project specification.
  14. The 28-day result is low. What do you do? Notify the designer immediately. The designer decides on re-check, cores or strengthening.
  15. Which grout goes under base plates? Non-shrink grout to ASTM C1107; for gaps over 50 mm, a coarse-aggregate or epoxy grout.

Questions engineers ask

What do QA/QC interviews ask about anchor bolts?
Template method, grades, tolerances, corrections for misaligned bolts, the pre-pour final gate, cube testing, and who approves field fixes.
What should you never do with a misaligned anchor bolt?
Bend or heat it without the structural engineer's approval.
Who approves anchor bolt field fixes?
The structural engineer.
