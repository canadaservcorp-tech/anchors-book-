Steel structure and welding inspector certifications: CWI, CSWIP 3.1, CWB, ASNT, AMPP

By Hicham Zein Eddine · Steel inspection reference note · the guide itself covers the foundation stage

Short answer: The main credentials are AWS CWI (USA and many international projects), CSWIP 3.1 Welding Inspector (UK, Middle East, Asia), CWB inspector certification to CSA W178.2 (Canada), ASNT or ISO 9712 NDT levels for testing, and the AMPP (formerly NACE) Coating Inspector Program. The right one depends on where you work and which code the projects use.

What certifications do not cover

None of these teach the foundation stage: survey, anchor-bolt setting, pre-pour hold points and concrete testing. Most steel problems on site start there. That gap is what the Anchor Bolt Field Guide and the workshop cover.

Questions engineers ask

What certification does a steel structure inspector need?
It depends on the region and code: AWS CWI, CSWIP 3.1, CWB/CSA W178.2 in Canada, plus NDT or coating certifications for those tasks.
Is CSWIP 3.1 equivalent to AWS CWI?
Both certify welding inspectors; CSWIP 3.1 is more common on UK and Gulf projects and CWI on AWS D1.1 projects. Employers decide which they accept.
What is AMPP?
The Association for Materials Protection and Performance, formed from NACE International and SSPC; it runs the Coating Inspector Program.
