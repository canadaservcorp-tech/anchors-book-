Steel structure foundation sequence: from drawing to grouted base plate

By Hicham Zein Eddine · From Chapter 1 of the Anchor Bolt Field Guide

Short answer: For PEB buildings, steel frames, warehouses, towers, poles and canopies the foundation workflow is the same: verify the drawings, set out with the surveyor, make the site safe, excavate, prepare the trench bottom, lay the polyethylene sheet and blinding, build the footing formwork, fix the rebar, set the anchor bolts in a template, then place the base plate on levelling nuts and grout it, with QA/QC hold points throughout.

Whether you are building a pre-engineered building (PEB), a hot-rolled frame, a metal warehouse, a transmission tower, a telecom mast, a car-park shade or a lighting pole, the anchor-bolt workflow is the same. The bolts must be set to the exact pattern, at the precise level, with the correct embedment, and held rigidly during casting.

The sequence

  1. Before the site: read and verify the drawings.
  2. Setting out: the surveyor turns the drawing into points, lines and benchmarks on the ground.
  3. Safe access to the site.
  4. Excavation and spoil management.
  5. Preparing the trench bottom.
  6. Anti-termite soil treatment where the region requires it.
  7. Polyethylene sheet.
  8. Blinding (lean concrete), set to level with the surveyor.
  9. Footing formwork on the surveyor's new marks.
  10. Reinforcement.
  11. Anchor bolts in a template: pattern, level, embedment, hook orientation.
  12. Base plate and grouting: levelling nuts, plumb column, non-shrink grout.
  13. Post-installed anchors, only for repairs and additions.
  14. QA/QC hold points from pre-blinding to post-curing.
  15. Troubleshooting and field fixes.

These are the 15 chapters of the guide.

Casting before the steel arrives

Steel is fabricated in the workshop for weeks or months before delivery. Waiting for it before starting the foundations would cripple the schedule. An accurate template, made from the base-plate detail and later used to drill the plates, lets the foundations be cast first with the confidence that the steel will fit.

Questions engineers ask

What is the construction sequence for a PEB foundation?
Drawings, survey, excavation, trench bottom, polyethylene sheet, blinding, formwork, rebar, anchor bolts in a template, concrete, then base plate on levelling nuts and non-shrink grout, with QA/QC hold points throughout.
Can foundations be cast before the steel is delivered?
Yes, if the anchor bolts are set with an accurate template made from the base-plate detail. The same template is then used to drill the base plates.
Is the sequence different for towers and poles?
No. The anchor-bolt workflow is the same; uplift and overturning make embedment, hook orientation and grout even more critical.
