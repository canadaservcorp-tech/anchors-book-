Washers on anchor bolts: plate washers, hardened washers and spring washers

By Hicham Zein Eddine · From the author's live workshop

Short answer: Base plate holes for anchor bolts are oversized, so a standard washer is not enough: the drawings normally call for a thick plate washer over each hole, sometimes welded to the base plate. Hardened washers are used where specified. Spring or lock washers are not a standard anchor-bolt detail; use them only if the drawings specify them.

Washers are small, cheap and often forgotten until the inspector asks for them. On anchor bolts they matter more than on ordinary bolts, because the holes in the base plate are deliberately oversized.

Plate washers over oversized holes

  • Base plate holes are larger than the bolt, so a normal washer can sit partly over the hole.
  • The usual detail is a thick plate washer with a hole just larger than the bolt.
  • Where the bolts carry shear, the drawings may require the plate washer to be welded to the base plate. Follow the detail exactly.

Hardened washers

Hardened washers (such as ASTM F436) are used where the specification calls for them, for example under a turned element or with high-strength bolts. Check the material list, not the stock in the container.

Spring and lock washers

Spring washers are not part of a standard anchor-bolt detail. Where vibration or loosening is a concern, such as on poles and towers, the design normally uses a double nut or a specified tightening method. Use spring washers only if the drawings say so.

During casting

Two nuts and two washers per bolt hold the template: one set below, one above. These are setting washers, not the final connection hardware.

Questions engineers ask

What washer goes on an anchor bolt over a base plate?
Normally a thick plate washer, because the base plate holes are oversized. The size and thickness come from the drawings.
Are spring washers used on anchor bolts?
Not as a standard detail. Use them only if the drawings specify them; vibration is usually handled with double nuts or a specified tightening method.
Why are plate washers sometimes welded to the base plate?
So the bolts can take shear through the washer when the holes are oversized. Only when the drawings require it.
