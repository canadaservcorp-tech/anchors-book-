Questions

Who is this anchor-bolt training for?
QA/QC inspectors and engineers, site engineers, technicians, foremen and steel erection supervisors, and students in civil construction programs.
Is the training online or in person?
Both. Live on Zoom or Teams for any country, or in person in Montréal–Laval and elsewhere in Québec on request.
Which languages are available?
English, French and Arabic.
Is there a certificate?
Each participant receives the digital field guide and a certificate of attendance. The workshop does not grant accredited credits or PDH.
Is it a recorded course?
No. Every session is taught live, with time for your own site questions.
Does it cover anchor-bolt design?
No. Design belongs to the structural engineer. The training covers site execution: verification, setting out, installation, protection, inspection and corrections.
