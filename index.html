Who it is for

Site engineers, structural engineers, QA/QC inspectors, and engineering students who want to walk onto a site on day one knowing what to do.

What is inside

  • The complete site sequence: drawings, survey, excavation, blinding, rebar, anchor bolts, base plate, grouting.
  • The anchor-bolt template method: fabricate it, set embedment, orient L/J-bolts, hold the pattern rigid through the pour.
  • QA/QC: ten mandatory hold points and a pre-pour final-gate checklist.
  • Troubleshooting: misaligned bolts, damaged threads, low concrete strength, grout voids.
  • Standards behind every step: ACI, AISC, BS EN, ASTM, ISO.
  • Site politics: how to lead experienced crews and protect your word.

Works for every metal structure: PEB, hot-rolled frames, warehouses, transmission towers and poles, monopoles, car-park shades and canopies.
